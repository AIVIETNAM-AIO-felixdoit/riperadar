How it works

1 · Segment

Background colour is estimated from the frame border; Lab distance + saturation seed a GrabCut mask that separates every item.

2 · Measure

Per-item HSV analysis gives green/yellow/red share, and adaptive darkness + brown-hue detection maps bruises and sugar spots.

3 · Identify

Each crop goes through CLIP ViT-B/32 zero-shot on CPU: what it is and which condition (unripe → rotten) it looks like.

4 · Fuse & plan

Colour and CLIP scores are fused per produce type into a 0–100 ripeness index, shelf-life model, and a Gemini use-it-up plan.
